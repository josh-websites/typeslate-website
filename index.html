Turn e-paper into
a writer's deck.

MicroSlate turns the Seeed reTerminal Sticky and E1001 into dedicated writer's decks. Pair a Bluetooth keyboard and start typing. Your notes save to a microSD card, ready to send to your phone or sync to the cloud.

Meet MicroSlate Choose a device

Installs from your browser in about two minutes.

A screen that only writes

A writer's deck is a device made for one job. Turn it on, and your draft is waiting.

📄

Paper-like e-ink

Reads like printed paper, stays sharp in bright sunlight and is easy on your eyes through long sessions.

⌨️

Your keyboard, your language

Pair any Bluetooth LE keyboard and pick from 32 layouts, including German, Russian and US International.

📤

Words you can take anywhere

Notes are plain files. Send them to your phone over Wi-Fi, or sync them to OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox.
