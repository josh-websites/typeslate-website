Turn e-paper into
your writer's deck.

MicroSlate turns an e-paper screen into a dedicated writing machine. Pair a Bluetooth keyboard, open a note and start typing. Your notes save to a microSD card, ready to send to your phone over Wi-Fi or sync to OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox.

Built for one job: writing

Your laptop is a distraction machine. MicroSlate gives you a calm screen made for writing, so your attention stays on the page.

📄

Paper-like e-ink screen

Reads like printed paper, stays sharp in bright sunlight and is easy on your eyes through long sessions. The X4 Pro adds a frontlight for writing in the dark.

⌨️

Bring your own keyboard

Pair any Bluetooth LE keyboard. MicroSlate remembers up to 4 and reconnects fast, so one keyboard can switch between your deck and your PC.

🌍

Your language, your layout

32 keyboard layouts, including German, Russian and US International, with accented Latin and Cyrillic letters on screen. Tweak any key with the layout editor on the device. Spellcheck in US or UK English. Get layouts

🗂️

Plain files you own

Notes are .txt or Markdown files on the microSD card. Layouts and settings are plain-text files too, so you can read, back up and share them.

🖼️

Make it yours

Send your own pictures from your phone and the device shows them on its sleep screen when you put it down.

✍️

A real editor

Notes up to 85k words, undo and redo, keyboard shortcuts and optional autocorrect. Every save is protected: if the power cuts out mid-save, your last good copy is kept.

Two ways to get your words out

Keep everything offline and hand notes over when you want, or let them sync on their own. Use one, or both.

Install MicroSlate

Straight from your browser. Works on Windows and Mac in Chrome or Edge.

Chrome or Edge required. Your browser doesn't support Web Serial, which is needed to install. Please open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer.

1. Choose your device

2. Connect and install

  1. aConnect your reTerminal Sticky to your computer with a USB-C cable.
  2. bClick Install, then pick the device in the browser popup.
  3. cWait about two minutes. The device restarts into MicroSlate.
Version: 3.0.0
Not for China-market units. X4 Pro units sold in China are locked and won't accept MicroSlate. Units sold elsewhere, including the Developer Edition, install normally. X4 Pro support is experimental for now, so please tell us how it runs.
Check your device. Each device needs its own build. Make sure the device you picked above is the one plugged in.

MicroSlate 3.0 isn't released yet. This installer goes live with the release.

Your notes on the microSD card are not touched.

Installed the wrong build?+

Nothing is damaged. Pick the correct device above and install again.

If your browser can't find the device, or you'd like its original firmware back, these tools can help:

  • reTerminal E1001: Seeed's E1001 guide and Firmware Hub
  • reTerminal Sticky: Seeed's Sticky flashing guide
  • Xteink X4 Pro: Xteink Flash Tools (community-made)
  • Any of them: CrossPoint's Advanced Flash Controls

Your notes on the microSD card are safe either way.

After installing

  1. Insert a microSD card (FAT32 or exFAT, as it comes out of the box).
  2. Go to Settings → Bluetooth and pair your keyboard.
  3. Optional: Settings → Wi-Fi, then sign in to OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox by scanning the QR code.
  4. Open a note and start writing.

Free and open source. Code and release notes on GitHub.

Questions

Is MicroSlate free?+

Yes. MicroSlate is free, open-source firmware. You only pay for the device and keyboard.

Which keyboards work?+

Bluetooth LE keyboards, which covers most modern wireless keyboards, including the Logitech Keys-to-Go 2. These devices have no Classic Bluetooth radio, so some older keyboards won't connect.

Does it replace the device's original software?+

Yes. MicroSlate replaces the firmware the device ships with, so it becomes a dedicated writer's deck. Seeed publishes its own firmware tools if you ever want to go back.

Where are my notes stored?+

As plain .txt or .md files on the microSD card, so you can always read them on a computer. Cloud sync is optional.

I have an original Xteink X4. Can I use MicroSlate?+

Yes. MicroSlate 2.1, including a dual-boot edition with the CrossPoint e-reader, is below. MicroSlate 3.0 for the X4 is coming later.

Original Xteink X4

MicroSlate 2.1 for the original X4

For the original USB-C Xteink X4. Install it on its own, or alongside the CrossPoint e-reader as a dual-boot.

Device compatibility

MicroSlate 2.1 is built for the original Xteink X4 and installs by flashing over its USB-C port. It does not work on the newer X4 Pro. For the X4 Pro, use MicroSlate 3.0 above.

Chrome or Edge required. Your current browser doesn't support Web Serial, which is needed to flash the device. Please open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer.

After flashing

  1. Insert a FAT32-formatted MicroSD card.
  2. Power on — the device boots to the main menu.
  3. Go to Settings → Bluetooth and scan for your keyboard.
  4. Select your keyboard and press Enter to pair.
  5. Return to the main menu and start writing.
