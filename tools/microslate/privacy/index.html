MicroSlate Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

The short version

Your writing stays on your device's SD card. If you turn on cloud sync, your device sends your notes straight to your own OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox account. MicroSlate has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no server that stores your notes. We never see what you write.

1. Who this covers

MicroSlate is open-source writing firmware for e-paper devices, published by TypeSlate (typeslate.com). This policy covers the MicroSlate firmware, including its optional cloud sync and the small sign-in service described in section 4. The typeslate.com website and the TypeSlate Windows app have their own policy at typeslate.com/privacy.html.

2. What is stored on your device

  • Your notes are plain text files on the SD card, in the /notes folder.
  • Settings, such as display and sleep preferences, are kept on the device and on the SD card. Your cloud choices, such as which provider is active, are kept in the device's internal memory.
  • Sign-in tokens for a connected cloud, and the account name shown on screen, are kept in the device's internal memory. They are never written to the SD card.
  • Bluetooth keyboard pairing keys are kept in the device's internal memory.
  • Saved Wi-Fi networks (names and passwords) are kept in the device's internal memory. A backup copy is also kept on the SD card in the /microslate folder so your networks survive a firmware reinstall. Anyone who holds your SD card can read that backup, so treat the card like a key, and delete /microslate/wifi.json before lending or discarding it.

3. Cloud sync (optional)

Cloud sync is off until you sign in. When you sign in, your device talks directly to your chosen provider over an encrypted HTTPS connection and checks the provider's certificate. Your notes do not pass through any MicroSlate server. What MicroSlate can reach in each account:

ProviderWhat MicroSlate can access
OneDrive (personal Microsoft accounts)Only its own app folder, plus your display name so the screen can show which account is connected.
Google DriveOnly files and folders MicroSlate itself created (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see the rest of your Drive.
DropboxOnly its own app folder in your Dropbox.

What is sent: your note text and file names, and the requests needed to list, upload, download and delete notes in that folder. Deleting a note on one side can remove it on the other the next time you sync; the device keeps notes removed by the cloud in a trash folder on the SD card.

The provider's own privacy policy governs what it does with the files once they are in your account.

4. The sign-in service (Dropbox only)

Dropbox has no on-device sign-in code, so MicroSlate uses a small web service, hosted on Cloudflare, to hand a one-time authorization code from your phone to your device. It is used only while you sign in to Dropbox.

  • It holds the one-time code for at most 10 minutes, then deletes it. A sign-in that is never approved stores nothing.
  • The code is useless without a secret key that never leaves your device.
  • Your notes and your sign-in tokens never pass through it, before or after sign-in.
  • The service does not log or store anything else. As with any hosting provider, Cloudflare processes basic network information such as IP addresses to deliver the connection; Cloudflare's privacy policy applies to that.

OneDrive and Google Drive do not use this service. If it is unreachable, you can type the Dropbox code on the device instead.

5. What MicroSlate does not do

  • No accounts with MicroSlate or TypeSlate.
  • No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking in the firmware.
  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • No access to your notes by us, ever.

6. Third parties

If you use cloud sync, your provider's terms and privacy policy apply to your account: Microsoft, Google and Dropbox.

MicroSlate's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Your choices

  • Sign out in the device's cloud screen. This removes the sign-in from the device and clears its sync record.
  • Revoke access from the provider: Microsoft account permissions, Google account permissions or Dropbox connected apps.
  • Delete your cloud copy by deleting the MicroSlate folder in your account. Your notes on the SD card are not affected.
  • Your notes are yours. They are ordinary text files that you can copy, move or delete at any time.

8. Children

MicroSlate is a general writing tool and is not aimed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Changes that affect what the firmware sends will also be described in the release notes.
